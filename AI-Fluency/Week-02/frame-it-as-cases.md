# Case Study: DoseForecast Tracker

### The Problem
Medication adherence is difficult when patients rely on manual counts or simple calendar reminders that don't account for changing schedules, skipped doses, or staggered refills. Most medication apps just tell you to take a pill right now; they don't look ahead to tell you when your prescription bottle is actually going to hit empty based on your real-world usage patterns.

### What I Did & Decided
I designed and built DoseForecast Tracker to shift from reactive reminders to predictive inventory management. I built out the core logic using TypeScript and React to track active prescriptions, logging daily consumption against remaining quantities. Instead of building a heavy backend database right away, I prioritized a robust local-first state model using clean React hooks to keep things fast, private, and reliable. I decided to focus the UI entirely on a clean, at-a-glance dashboard that surfaces "days until empty" front and center rather than burying it under menu tabs.

### What Came of It
The app gives users an accurate, real-time forecast of their medication supply, transforming raw schedule data into an actionable warning system before a refill emergency happens. It stands as a practical, portfolio-ready tool that solves a genuine daily problem without unnecessary software bloat.
